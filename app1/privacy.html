1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how this app handles information. This app itself does not collect, transmit, or share any of the financial or personal information you enter into its calculators — that stays on your device (see Section 2). This app does, however, display advertising served by third-party advertising networks, which may collect some information as described in Section 4 below. Please read that section carefully.

2. Information You Enter Into the Calculators

This app lets you enter figures such as loan amounts, interest rates, savings goals, retirement balances, and debt details, and lets you save named plans built from that information. All of this is stored locally on your device only, using standard on-device storage. This app does not upload, transmit, back up, or share this calculator information with the developer(s) or any third party. You can delete this information at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app from your device's settings, which removes it immediately and permanently — see the Terms of Use & Disclaimer for details on the lack of any backup.

3. No Accounts, No Personal Information Collected by Us

This app does not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide your name, email address, or any other personal identifying information to use the app, and the developer(s) do not directly collect any such information.

4. Advertising and Third-Party Data Collection

This app is a free app supported by advertising, served through Google AdMob, which operates independently of this app and is not controlled by the developer(s). AdMob and its partner ad networks may automatically collect information to select, deliver, and measure ads, such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address, general (non-precise) location, device type, and how you interact with ads — separately from, and unrelated to, any financial figures you type into the calculators, which AdMob does not have access to. This information is collected and processed under Google's own privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and its partners' policies, not this one. Where required by law (for example, for users in the European Economic Area and the UK), the app shows a consent form before loading any ads, using Google's User Messaging Platform, so you can choose whether to allow personalized advertising. You can change that choice later from the Legal menu on the Home screen. On iOS, if ads are personalized based on activity across other companies' apps and websites, Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework separately requires the app to ask for your permission first; you can allow or deny this at any time in your device's Settings. On both iOS and Android, you can generally reset or limit your device's advertising identifier, or opt out of personalized advertising, in your device's system settings.

5. Data Retention

This app retains the calculator information described in Section 2 for as long as it remains stored on your device — that is, until you delete it yourself, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app. This app has no ability to retain, back up, or delete this information remotely, because it is never transmitted to us in the first place. For information collected by third-party advertising networks (see Section 4), retention is governed by those networks' own policies, not this one.

6. Security of Your Information

Because this app does not operate servers and does not transmit your calculator information anywhere, there is no centralized database of your data for us to secure, and no server-side system of ours that could be breached. The security of information stored on your device depends on your own device's security (such as your device lock screen, operating system updates, and any device encryption you have enabled). We are not responsible for information lost or exposed due to the loss, theft, or compromise of your device.

7. European Economic Area / UK Privacy Rights (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA) or the United Kingdom, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the UK GDPR give you rights over personal data processed about you. Because this app does not itself collect, transmit, or store any personal data about you outside of your own device (see Sections 2 and 3), this app has very little personal data of its own to process as a "controller." To the extent this app is a controller of any personal data (for example, an email address you voluntarily send us through the Feedback feature or by email), that processing is based on your consent (given by contacting us) or our legitimate interest in responding to you.

Where applicable, you have the right to: request access to personal data we hold about you; request correction of inaccurate data; request erasure of your data; request that we restrict or object to certain processing; request a portable copy of your data; and lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority. Because this app holds no personal data beyond what you may voluntarily send us by email, most of these requests will have little or nothing to act on — but you can still contact us using the information in the Contact section below to ask.

The advertising described in Section 4 is a separate matter: Google acts as an independent controller for the data its ad networks collect, under its own privacy policy and its own legal bases (including your consent, gathered through the in-app consent form for EEA/UK users). Requests about that data should generally be directed to Google.

This app does not operate an establishment in the EEA or UK and, because it does not process personal data as a controller beyond the limited, occasional circumstances described above, has not appointed an EU or UK representative under GDPR Article 27 / UK GDPR.

8. California Privacy Rights (CCPA/CPRA)

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), gives you certain rights regarding personal information collected about you — including the right to know what personal information is collected, the right to request deletion of personal information, the right to correct inaccurate personal information, and the right to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information. This app itself does not sell personal information. However, the advertising described in Section 4 above (personalized advertising served through Google AdMob and its partner networks) may be considered a "share" of personal information under the CPRA's broad definition. Where available, the app's ad consent flow and your device's ad settings let you opt out of personalized advertising, which limits this sharing. To exercise other CCPA/CPRA rights, contact us using the information in the Contact section below — because this app itself does not collect or retain personal information beyond what's described above, most such requests will need to be directed to the relevant advertising network (see Section 4) for data it independently collects.

This app provides the rights described in this section as a matter of transparency and good practice, regardless of whether the CCPA/CPRA's "business" thresholds (based on revenue or the number of consumers' data processed) currently apply to an app of this size. Global Privacy Control and similar browser-based opt-out signals are designed for websites and do not have a direct mobile-app equivalent; this app instead lets you express the same opt-out choice directly through the in-app ad consent flow described in Section 4.

9. Other U.S. State Privacy Rights

Beyond California, a number of other U.S. states — including Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Utah, and others — have their own consumer privacy laws granting residents rights similar to those described in the California section above: typically the right to access, correct, delete, and obtain a portable copy of personal data, and the right to opt out of the sale, sharing, or targeted-advertising use of personal data. As with the California rights above, this app itself collects very little personal data (see Sections 2 and 3), so most of these rights have little to act on directly — but you can still contact us using the information in the Contact section below, and where advertising-related data is involved, the same AdMob consent flow described in Section 4 applies regardless of which state you're in.

10. No Other Analytics or Tracking

Outside of the advertising described in Section 4, this app does not include any additional analytics or tracking software, and does not use cookies or similar technologies of its own. The on-device storage described in Section 2 is not a "cookie" in the traditional web sense — it is local storage private to your device, never transmitted anywhere, and is not used for tracking.

11. App Store Platforms

This policy covers this app itself. The app store you downloaded this app from (Apple App Store or Google Play) may independently collect certain information as part of operating its platform (such as download statistics or crash reports), under its own privacy policy, which this app does not control.

12. Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children and is not intended to be used by anyone under the age of 13 (or the age of digital consent in your jurisdiction, if higher). We do not knowingly direct advertising to children. The app itself collects no personal information from anyone, child or adult (see Sections 2 and 3), so there is no children's data collected through use of the app for us to access, retain, or delete. If a message that appears to be from a child under this age reaches us directly — for example, through the Feedback feature or by email — we will delete it upon becoming aware, without retaining or using its contents for any other purpose.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, including to reflect changes in the advertising networks we use. Continued use of the app after such changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
